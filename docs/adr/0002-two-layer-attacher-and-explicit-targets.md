# Two-Layer Attacher Core, Explicit Targets, and Pruning Tautological Options

In 4.4, `ember-attacher` introduces a shared `AttacherCore` backing three public entry points—`{{tooltip "Text" class="..."}}` (backed by a shared singleton **Attachment** pool), `<Attacher as |a|>` (headless primitive), and `<AttachTooltip>` / `<AttachPopover>` (supporting `@target`, yielded `{{a.target data}}`, `<:target>`/`<:content>` blocks, and `[popovertarget]` pairing)—while deprecating (`until: '5.0.0'`) implicit parent-finding and tautological 4.x arguments (`@explicitTarget`, `reference`/`setReference`, `@flip`, `@overflowPadding`, `@floatingUiOptions`, `@floatingElementContainer`, `@isOffset`, `@style`, `@additionalClass`, `@onInitializeAttacher`, `@onWillDestroy`) for removal in 5.0.

We rejected (a) dropping `<AttachTooltip>` and `<AttachPopover>` in favor of `<Attacher @as="...">` alone, which would cause unnecessary template churn across existing callers, and (b) defaulting missing **Targets** to `document.body` in 5.0, which breaks `clickout`, attaches page-wide **Triggers**, pollutes `body[aria-describedby]`, and fails in SSR. In 5.0, missing **Targets** assert in debug builds and remain inert in production and SSR.
